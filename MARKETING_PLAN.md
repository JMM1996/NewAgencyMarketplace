## Executive Summary

NewAgencyMarketplace is entering the UK care staffing market with a digital-first solution for short-term and emergency staffing needs. This marketing plan outlines a lean, phased approach to acquiring both sides of our marketplace: **care homes** (demand) and **self-employed care workers** (supply).

### Core Marketing Objectives

| Phase | Timeline | Primary Goal |
|-------|----------|--------------|
| Pre-Launch | Feb - April 2026 | Build waitlist, validate demand |
| MVP Launch | May 2026 | Acquire 5 care homes, 10 care workers |
| Growth | June - Sept 2026 | Scale to 50+ bookings |
| Monetisation | Oct - Dec 2026 | Establish paid acquisition channels |

### The Chicken-and-Egg Challenge

As a two-sided marketplace, we must solve the classic supply-demand problem:

**Our Strategy:** Lead with supply (care workers) to create a compelling value proposition for care homes. A care home will only join if there are workers available; workers will stay if there are shifts to fill.

## Market Analysis

### UK Care Sector Overview

- **1.5 million** people work in adult social care in England
- **165,000** staff vacancies across the sector (11% vacancy rate)
- **30%** annual staff turnover in care homes
- Care homes spend an estimated **£1.5 billion annually** on agency staff

### Market Pain Points

**For Care Homes:**
- Traditional agencies charge **50-100% markups** on hourly rates
- Last-minute cancellations leave shifts unfilled
- Long procurement processes for agency contracts
- Quality inconsistency with agency staff

**For Care Workers:**
- Limited flexibility with permanent roles
- Agency work offers less take-home pay due to agency cuts
- Difficulty finding local, short-term opportunities
- No direct relationship with care homes

### Market Opportunity

The intersection of:
- High vacancy rates creating urgent demand
- Growing gig economy acceptance among workers
- Digital transformation lagging in care sector
- Cost pressure on care homes seeking alternatives to agencies

## Value Proposition & Messaging

### Core Value Proposition

> **For care homes:** Fill short-term shifts quickly with local, qualified care workers - at a fraction of traditional agency costs.

> **For care workers:** Find flexible shifts near you, set your own rates, and keep more of what you earn.

### Messaging Framework

#### For Care Homes

| Message Pillar | Headline | Supporting Points |
|----------------|----------|-------------------|
| **Speed** | "Shifts filled in hours, not days" | Instant access to local workers; Real-time availability; No lengthy agency contracts |
| **Cost** | "Save 20%+ vs traditional agencies" | Transparent 15% fee; No hidden costs; Direct worker rates |
| **Quality** | "Build your trusted worker pool" | See profiles and experience; Rate workers after shifts; Favourites feature (Phase 3) |
| **Simplicity** | "Post a shift in 60 seconds" | Simple interface; No paperwork burden; Automated confirmations |

#### For Care Workers

| Message Pillar | Headline | Supporting Points |
|----------------|----------|-------------------|
| **Flexibility** | "Work when and where you want" | Browse local shifts; Accept what suits you; No minimum commitments |
| **Earnings** | "Keep more of what you earn" | No agency middleman taking 30%+; Set your own rates; Get paid directly |
| **Opportunity** | "Access shifts traditional agencies don't share" | Local care homes posting directly; Emergency cover opportunities; Build relationships |
| **Simplicity** | "Apply in one tap" | Mobile-friendly; Quick profile setup; Instant notifications |

### Tagline Options

- "Care staffing, simplified"
- "The smarter way to fill care shifts"
- "Where care homes meet great carers"
- "Flexible care work, on your terms"

**Recommended:** "Care staffing, simplified" - works for both audiences

## Brand Positioning

### Brand Personality

- **Professional** but not corporate
- **Warm** but not sentimental
- **Modern** but accessible
- **Trustworthy** and transparent

### Tone of Voice

- Clear and jargon-free
- Supportive and understanding of sector challenges
- Confident but not arrogant
- Action-oriented

### Visual Identity Guidelines (Recommendations)

- **Primary colours:** Calming blues and greens (trust, care, health)
- **Typography:** Clean, readable sans-serif (accessibility important)
- **Imagery:** Real care settings, diverse workers, warm interactions
- **Avoid:** Stock photos that feel clinical or corporate

### Positioning Statement

> NewAgencyMarketplace is the modern alternative to traditional care staffing agencies. We connect care homes directly with qualified, self-employed care workers for short-term and emergency cover - faster, simpler, and more cost-effective than traditional agencies.

## Channel Strategy

### Phase 1: Pre-Launch & MVP (Feb - May 2026)

**Goal:** Build waitlist of 20 care homes and 50 care workers

#### Supply Side (Care Workers)

| Channel | Tactic | Budget | Expected Results |
|---------|--------|--------|------------------|

| **LinkedIn Outreach** | Connect with care professionals, share platform vision | £0 (time only) | 5 sign-ups |

| Channel | Tactic | Budget | Expected Results |
|---------|--------|--------|------------------|
| **Cold Email** | Targeted outreach to care home managers in pilot areas (personalised, value-led) | £50 (email tool) | 5 meetings |

| **Local Networks** | Attend local care provider forums and networking events | £100 (travel) | 2 sign-ups |
| **Referrals** | Ask early adopters for introductions to other care homes | £0 | Ongoing |

**Total Pre-Launch Budget:** ~£500

## Phased Marketing Timeline

### Phase 0: Foundation (Now - Early Feb 2026)

- [ ] Finalise brand identity and messaging
- [ ] Set up social media accounts (LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram)
- [ ] Create landing page with waitlist capture
- [ ] Begin building email list
- [ ] Join relevant Facebook groups and LinkedIn communities
- [ ] Create founder LinkedIn presence as industry voice

### Phase 1: Pre-Launch (Feb - April 2026)

**Month 1 (February):**
- Launch landing page with waitlist
- Begin cold outreach to care homes in pilot area
- Post in 5 care worker Facebook groups per week
- Create 4 pieces of content for social sharing

**Month 2 (March):**
- Host virtual "founder chat" with interested care homes
- Launch Indeed job board presence
- Begin LinkedIn content cadence (3x/week)
- Reach: 10 care homes interested, 30 care workers signed up

**Month 3 (April):**
- Invite top waitlist members to beta test
- Gather testimonials from early users
- Prepare launch announcement materials
- Target: 20 care homes, 50 care workers on waitlist

### Phase 2: MVP Launch (May 2026)

**Launch Week:**
- Email blast to full waitlist
- Social media launch announcement
- Personal outreach to committed early adopters
- Goal: 5 care homes posting shifts, 10 workers applying

**Weeks 2-4:**
- Monitor and support first bookings
- Document success stories
- Iterate on messaging based on user feedback
- Begin paid acquisition testing (small budget)

### Phase 3: Growth (June - Sept 2026)

- Scale paid acquisition on proven channels
- Expand geographic targeting
- Launch referral programme
- Publish first case studies
- Monthly target: 20% growth in both supply and demand

### Phase 4: Scale (Oct - Dec 2026)

- Attend first trade event
- Launch PR campaign
- Expand content marketing
- Test partnership opportunities
- Prepare for monetisation messaging (payment integration)

## Competitive Differentiation

### Competitive Landscape

| Competitor | Model | Our Advantage |
|------------|-------|---------------|
| **Traditional Agencies** (Pulse, Mayday) | High markup (50-100%), contract-based | Lower cost, no contracts, faster |
| **Florence** | Nurse-focused, larger scale | Care worker focus, local relationships |
| **Caring.com / Care.com** | US-focused, consumer care | UK-specific, B2B focus |
| **Indeed/Jobs boards** | General job listings | Specialised for care, shift-based |
| **WhatsApp groups** | Informal, unstructured | Organised, verified, trackable |

### Our Unique Advantages

1. **Cost Transparency:** Clear 15% fee vs hidden agency markups
2. **Speed:** Digital-first means shifts filled in hours
3. **Relationship Building:** Workers and homes build direct relationships
4. **Simplicity:** Purpose-built for care, not a general platform
5. **Local Focus:** Starting hyper-local, understanding regional needs

### Messaging Against Competitors

**vs Traditional Agencies:**
> "Why pay agency markups when you can connect directly? Our transparent 15% fee means better rates for workers and lower costs for care homes."

**vs General Job Boards:**
> "Stop scrolling through irrelevant jobs. Find care shifts near you, from care homes that need you now."

**vs Informal Networks:**
> "All the flexibility of WhatsApp groups, with the reliability of a proper platform. Verified workers, confirmed bookings, no confusion."
