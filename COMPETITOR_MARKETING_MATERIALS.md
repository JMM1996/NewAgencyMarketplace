## Florence (Market Leader)

### Headlines & Taglines
- "Fill shifts with people you trust. Only with Florence."
- "No ringing round. No hassle. Total peace of mind."
- "Simpler than WhatsApp, without the back-and-forth"
- "Take control, save money and spend less time organising your staff"
- "Designed to save you time, stress and money"

### Key Statistics (Use These as Benchmarks)
| Stat | Value | Context |
|------|-------|---------|
| Agency cost savings | **30%** | "Care homes typically save 30% on their agency spend" |
| Fill rate (advance booking) | **94%** | Shifts posted 1 week in advance |
| Vetted professionals | **90,000+** | Nurses, HCAs, support workers |
| Care homes signed up | **600+** | As of 2019 |
| Shifts worked | **500,000+** | April 2017 - March 2019 |

### Value Propositions (To Care Homes)
1. **Cost reduction**: "Save 30% on agency spend"
2. **Quality control**: "Rate all Florence nurses and carers after every shift"
3. **Relationship building**: "Favourites feature" to build trusted worker bank
4. **Simplicity**: "No tech skills needed"
5. **24/7 support**: Dedicated team available continuously
6. **Tiered staffing**: Auto-fill with permanent staff first, then agency

### Case Study: Four Seasons Health Care Group
**Challenge**: Managing 250+ agencies across 46 care homes was complex and costly.

**Results with Florence**:
- **£2.1 million saved** on agency staffing
- Reduced agencies from **250 to 50**
- Greater control and transparency

**Quote**: *"We needed to take back control of our temporary staffing agencies, gain greater transparency and control of costs."*

### Messaging to Workers
- "Find flexible, fair, and transparent work options"
- "Work when you want, where you want"
- Access to NHS and social care shifts
- "Instant Pay" - paid same day after timesheet approval

## Curam Care

### Headlines & Taglines
- "No agency mark-ups, hidden fees or exit charges"
- "Clients pay on average 22% less than the current suggested UK Home Care Association hourly rate"
- "Vetted & Trusted Network"
- "Matching, not placement"

### Key Statistics
| Stat | Value |
|------|-------|
| Average carer experience | **10 years** |
| Savings vs UKHCA rate | **22%** |
| Commission fee | **12.5% + VAT** |
| Permanent hire fee | **£750 + VAT** (after 90 days) |

### Value Propositions
1. **Transparency**: "No hidden costs" - platform fee included in quoted prices
2. **Control**: Clients negotiate rates directly with carers
3. **Trust**: DBS checked, insured, biometric ID verification "to banking standards"
4. **Flexibility**: Post needs for carers to apply OR browse/search carers directly
5. **Cost comparison**: Live-in care cheaper than nursing homes

### Pricing Messaging
```
"Transparent pricing with no agency mark-ups"
"You agree your rate directly with a self-employed caregiver"
"12% platform fee is included in quoted prices" (Note: Actually 12.5% + VAT)
```

### Positioning Statement
*"A service for clients to find local, self-employed caregivers. This innovative model makes a difference to clients and the carers we work with."*

### Headlines & Taglines
- "High-quality care at affordable prices"
- "Choice, transparency and control"
- "Reshape the care industry"
- "Flexibility without paying through the nose for it"
- "Over 500,000 hours of care" delivered since 2015

### Pricing Tiers
| Service | Price | Key Difference |
|---------|-------|----------------|
| Introductory Care | From £1,400/week | You manage the care |
| Regulated Care | From £1,500/week | Fully managed clinical service |

1. **Cost savings**: Hourly rates from £10/hr (vs £20+ via agency)
2. **Vetting**: "Interview every carer before uploading"
3. **Choice**: Informed carer selection by skills/experience/location
4. **Transparency**: Rates negotiated upfront, "no hidden costs"
5. **Dual model**: Both introductory AND regulated care options

### Comparison Messaging
| Factor | Agencies | Hometouch |
|--------|----------|-----------|
| Commission | High markups | "Low commission" |
| Carer selection | "Limited, if any, choice" | Informed choice |
| Pricing | Hidden costs | Upfront negotiation |

### Trust Signals
- CQC regulated
- Only 5% of carers accepted
- Accredited Living Wage employer

## Messaging Gaps You Can Exploit

### 1. Self-Employment Focus
Florence moved to PAYE. No competitor strongly owns the **"stay self-employed"** message for care homes.

**Your angle**: *"True self-employment. Direct contracts. No IR35 headaches."*

### 2. Care Home Specificity
Curam and Hometouch target private/home care. Florence does both NHS and care homes.

**Your angle**: *"Built specifically for care homes. Nothing else."*

### 3. Simplicity
All competitors have added complexity (training, bank management, agency management, regulated care tiers).

**Your angle**: *"Post a shift. Get it filled. That's it."*

### 4. Transparent Flat Fee
Competitors have variable/hidden fees or complex pricing.

**Your angle**: *"15% flat fee. Always. No surprises."*
