## Pricing & Costs

### How much does it cost?

> **Answer:** We charge a 15% platform fee on top of the worker's rate. So if a worker charges £15/hour, you pay £17.25/hour. There are no hidden fees, no contracts, no minimums. You only pay when you use a worker.
>
> **Comparison:** Traditional agencies typically charge £18-25/hour for HCAs (that's a 50-100% markup on what the worker actually earns). So even though you're paying a competitive rate, you're still saving about 20%.

### Why should I believe you're cheaper than agencies?

> **Answer:** Agencies have recruiters, offices, account managers, and shareholders to pay. We're a technology platform - we've cut out those overheads. We can afford to take 15% and still build a good business. The worker earns more, you pay less. That's the maths.
>
> **Proof point:** Let's do the calculation with your actual agency rates right now. [Use ROI calculator]

### What's the catch?

> **Answer:** No catch. We make money when you use the platform. If workers are reliable and you keep posting shifts, we do well. Our incentives are aligned - we want your shifts filled with good workers just as much as you do.

### Do I need to sign a contract?

> **Answer:** No contracts, no minimums, no commitment. You can post one shift, or a hundred. Pay as you go. If you don't use us again, that's fine (though we'd be sad).

## Worker Quality & Verification

### How do I know the workers are any good?

> **Answer:** Three things:
> 1. You see their full profile before accepting - experience, qualifications, availability
> 2. They've chosen to apply for your shift specifically (unlike agency temps who are sent wherever)
> 3. Over time, you'll build relationships with regulars who know your home
>
> **Reality check:** No system is perfect - you'll occasionally get someone who isn't right. The difference is you don't have to use them again, and you're not paying agency markups for that trial.

### Are workers DBS checked?

> **Answer:** Workers declare their DBS status when they sign up, and you can see this in their profile. Currently, it's self-declared (same as most agencies to be honest). We're working on verified badges for future phases.
>
> **Your responsibility:** You should still follow your normal processes for checking ID and right-to-work when a new worker arrives - same as you would with agency staff.

### What qualifications do workers have?

> **Answer:** It varies - some are experienced HCAs with NVQs, others are newer to care. All profiles show their experience and any qualifications. You choose based on what you need for each shift.

### What about insurance?

> **Answer:** Workers on our platform are self-employed and must have their own public liability insurance. This is standard for self-employed care workers. They're not employed by us or by you - they're independent contractors providing a service.

## How It Works

### How quickly can shifts be filled?

> **Answer:** Most shifts are filled within a few hours if posted 24-48 hours ahead. Same-day emergency shifts are possible but depend on who's available. The more advance notice, the better your options.

### What if no one applies?

> **Answer:** It can happen, especially for very last-minute shifts or unsociable hours. We only launch in areas where we have good worker coverage - in your postcode we have [X] workers. If a shift doesn't get filled, you don't pay anything.
>
> **Tip:** For the best results, post shifts as early as possible and offer competitive rates.

### Can I request specific workers?

> **Answer:** Once you've worked with someone and liked them, you can favourite them (coming in a future update). For now, you can indicate in your shift description that you're looking for someone who's worked with you before, and they'll often apply.

### What shifts can I post?

> **Answer:** Any short-term or temporary shifts - days, nights, weekends, bank holidays. Most common are 8 or 12-hour shifts, but you set the hours. Minimum is usually 4 hours (workers won't apply for less).

### What roles do you cover?

> **Answer:** Right now, mainly HCAs and support workers with care home experience. We're expanding to cover seniors and nurses. Let me check what's available in your area. [Show platform]

## Legal & Compliance

### Who employs the workers?

> **Answer:** No one - they're self-employed. They're not our employees and not yours. This is an important distinction. They invoice for their services (or we handle payment for them in a future version), and they're responsible for their own tax and National Insurance.

### What about CQC compliance?

> **Answer:** Using self-employed workers for temporary cover is common and CQC-compliant. You should still follow your own procedures for checking ID, qualifications, and right-to-work when someone new arrives. We provide the platform and information; you manage compliance as you would normally.

### What happens if something goes wrong during a shift?

> **Answer:** If there's an issue with a worker's performance, you handle it directly as you would with any temporary staff. Don't use them again and let us know so we can note it on their profile. For serious incidents, workers can be removed from the platform. For liability issues, workers have their own insurance.

### What about IR35?

> **Answer:** Because workers are genuinely self-employed - they set their own rates, choose their own shifts, can work for multiple homes, and you're not supervising or controlling them as you would an employee - IR35 shouldn't be a concern. The platform model is designed for genuine self-employment, not disguised employment.
>
> (Standard caveat: we're not lawyers - if you're concerned, get professional advice)
