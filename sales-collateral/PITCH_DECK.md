## APPENDIX: Objection Responses

Keep these handy during Q&A:

### "We already use an agency"

> "Most of our customers do too - they use us alongside agencies for overflow and cost savings. On average, they save 20% on the shifts they fill through us. What do you currently pay per hour through your agency?"

### "We don't have time to try something new"

> "I totally understand - that's exactly why we've made it so simple. You can post your first shift in 60 seconds. Would it be worth 5 minutes to see if it could save you hours each week?"

### "How do I know the workers are any good?"

> "Great question. Every worker has a full profile with their experience, qualifications, and availability. You see everything before you accept them - unlike agency workers who just turn up. Plus, you're building relationships with local people who want to work with you regularly."

### "What if no one applies to my shift?"

> "We only launch in areas where we have good worker coverage. In your postcode, we currently have X workers available. We also offer a satisfaction guarantee on your first 3 shifts - if you're not happy, we'll waive the fee."

### "It's too cheap - what's the catch?"

> "No catch at all. We're a technology platform, not a traditional agency with offices, recruiters, and middle managers. Those are the costs we've cut out. We pass the savings to you and the workers - which is why we attract quality people."

### "I need to speak to my owner/director"

> "Of course. Would it be helpful if I put together a one-page summary you can share with them? I can also include the ROI calculation based on your current agency spend. When would be a good time to follow up after you've spoken?"

### "Can you send me more information?"

> "Absolutely. What specifically would be most useful? I can send you a summary, some case studies, and a link to sign up when you're ready. What's the best email to send that to?"

## PRESENTATION TIPS

1. **Personalise it** - Use their name, their care home name, their postcode throughout

2. **Listen more than you talk** - Ask questions, note their answers, use them later

3. **Do the maths with them** - Don't just show generic savings, calculate their specific savings

4. **Demo if possible** - Seeing the platform is more powerful than slides

5. **Address objections before they raise them** - The "workers" slide pre-empts "are they any good?"

6. **Always end with a clear next step** - Never end a meeting without agreeing what happens next

7. **Follow up same day** - Send a thank you email within 2 hours with anything you promised
