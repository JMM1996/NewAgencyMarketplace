## Sequence 1: Standard Cold Outreach (5 Emails)

### Email 1: Initial Outreach

**Subject lines (A/B test):**
- A: Quick question about staffing at [Care Home Name]
- B: Cutting agency costs at [Care Home Name]?
- C: [First Name], 60 seconds to fill your next shift

**Body:**

```
Hi [First Name],

I'll keep this short - I know you're busy.

I'm reaching out because I've been speaking with care home managers in [Area]
who all share the same frustration: agency costs are eating into margins,
and last-minute cover is still hit or miss.

We've built something that might help. NewAgencyMarketplace connects you
directly with local, verified care workers for short-term shifts - at about
20% less than traditional agencies.

The difference? You see full worker profiles before accepting, and you're
building relationships with people who want to come back - not random temps.

Would a quick 15-minute call be useful to see if it's a fit for [Care Home Name]?

Best,
[Your Name]

P.S. We're offering founding partners their first 3 shifts with 0% platform fee.
```

**Send:** Day 1
**Best time:** Tuesday-Thursday, 10am or 2pm

## Email Best Practices

### Subject Line Tips
- Keep under 50 characters
- Use their name or care home name
- Create curiosity, not clickbait
- A/B test every sequence

### Body Copy Tips
- Keep paragraphs to 2-3 lines max
- Use bullet points for scanability
- One clear call-to-action per email
- Write like a human, not a corporation

### Timing
- **Best days:** Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday
- **Best times:** 10:00-11:00am, 2:00-3:00pm
- **Avoid:** Monday morning, Friday afternoon, weekends

### Personalisation Checklist
- [ ] First name
- [ ] Care home name
- [ ] Location/area
- [ ] Specific pain point (if known)
- [ ] Recent CQC report reference (if relevant)
- [ ] Referrer name (if applicable)
- [ ] Custom ROI calculation (if sent)

### Follow-Up Cadence
```
Day 1:  Email 1 (Initial outreach)
Day 3:  Email 2 (Value-add)
Day 6:  Email 3 (Social proof)
Day 10: Email 4 (Direct ask)
Day 14: Email 5 (Break-up)
Day 30: Re-engagement sequence (if no response)
```

### Tracking Metrics

| Metric | Target | Action if Below |
|--------|--------|-----------------|
| Open rate | 40%+ | Improve subject lines |
| Reply rate | 10%+ | Improve body copy/CTA |
| Meeting rate | 5%+ | Improve value proposition |
