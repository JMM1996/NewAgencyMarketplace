## Design Notes for Print/PDF Version

### Layout
- **Size:** A4 single page (one side)
- **Orientation:** Portrait
- **Margins:** 15mm all sides

### Colours
- **Primary:** Deep blue (#1E3A5F) - headings, key text
- **Accent:** Teal/green (#2D9596) - icons, highlights
- **Background:** White with light grey (#F5F5F5) boxes
- **Text:** Dark grey (#333333)

### Typography
- **Headings:** Bold sans-serif (e.g., Inter, Poppins)
- **Body:** Regular sans-serif, 10-11pt
- **Key numbers:** Large, bold, accent colour

### Visual Elements
- Simple icons for each benefit (use Heroicons or similar)
- Logo prominent at top
- QR code linking to sign-up page (bottom right)
- Clear visual hierarchy - scannable in 30 seconds

### Print Specifications
- Paper: 170gsm silk or matt
- Quantity: Start with 200 for door-to-door visits
- Consider branded folder to hold multiple materials

## Usage Guidelines

### When to Use
- Leave behind after door-to-door visits
- Attach to follow-up emails after cold calls
- Hand out at networking events
- Include in any physical mailouts
- Share with decision-makers who weren't in the meeting

### Customisation Options
- Add local worker count for specific postcodes
- Include local testimonial when available
- Adjust savings example to match local agency rates
