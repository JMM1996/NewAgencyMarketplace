## Quick Start for New Sales Reps

### Day 1: Read & Familiarise
1. Read the [Marketing Plan](../MARKETING_PLAN.md) for strategy context
2. Read all documents in this folder
3. Memorise the Quick Stats (bottom of FAQ_SHEET.md)
4. Practice the elevator pitch from CALL_SCRIPTS.md

### Week 1: Shadowing & Practice
1. Shadow experienced reps on calls
2. Role-play cold calls and discovery calls
3. Customise email templates with your details
4. Get comfortable with the ROI calculator

### Ongoing: Refine & Improve
1. A/B test email subject lines
2. Note which objection responses work best
3. Collect customer quotes for social proof
4. Share winning tactics with the team
