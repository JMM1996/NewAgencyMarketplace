# Call Scripts - NewAgencyMarketplace

## Phone Scripts for Care Home Sales

---

## Cold Calling Script

### Objective
Book a 15-20 minute discovery call or demo, NOT sell on the first call.

### Best Times to Call
- **10:00 - 11:00am** (after morning handover, before lunch)
- **2:00 - 3:00pm** (quieter afternoon period)
- **Avoid:** 7-9am (shift change), 12-1pm (lunch), after 4pm (end of day)

---

### Gatekeeper Script

**When reception/admin answers:**

```
You: "Hi, this is [Your Name]. Could I speak with the registered manager, please?"

[If asked what it's about:]

You: "It's about staffing support for the care home. Is [Manager Name] available?"

[If they ask for more details:]

You: "We help care homes fill short-term shifts more quickly and affordably.
It'll only take two minutes - is now a good time to put me through?"

[If manager isn't available:]

You: "No problem. What time would be best to catch them? And could I get
their name and direct line if possible? ... Great, I'll try again at [time].
Thank you!"
```

**Tips:**
- Be confident and direct - you have something valuable
- Don't over-explain to the gatekeeper
- Get the manager's name if you don't have it
- Note the best callback time

---

### Main Cold Call Script

**Opening (10 seconds - pattern interrupt)**

```
You: "Hi [First Name], this is [Your Name] from NewAgencyMarketplace.
I know you're busy, so I'll be quick. Is now a terrible time?"

[Wait for response]
```

**Why this works:**
- Acknowledges they're busy (respect)
- "Terrible time" is unexpected - pattern interrupt
- Gives them control, reduces resistance

---

**If they say "Now's not a good time":**

```
You: "Totally understand. When would be better - later today or tomorrow morning?"

[Book specific callback time]

You: "Great, I'll call you at [time]. Just so you know what it's about - we
help care homes fill last-minute shifts faster and cheaper than agencies.
Talk to you [day]."
```

---

**If they give you time (30-60 seconds max):**

```
You: "Appreciate it. Quick question: how much of a headache is finding
last-minute cover when staff call in sick?"

[Let them answer - this is key discovery information]

You: "Yeah, I hear that a lot. That's actually why I'm calling. We've built
a platform that connects care homes directly with local care workers for
short-term shifts - typically about 20% cheaper than agencies, and most
shifts get filled within a few hours.

Quick question: would it be worth a 15-minute call to see if it could
work for [Care Home Name]? I can show you exactly how many workers are
available near you."

[Wait for response]
```

---

**If they say YES:**

```
You: "Great! What works better for you - [give two specific options, e.g.,
'Thursday morning or Friday afternoon']?"

[Book the meeting]

You: "Perfect, I've got [Day] at [Time]. I'll send you a calendar invite
with a video link. Looking forward to it - and I promise to keep it to
15 minutes. Talk soon!"
```

---

**If they say NO / NOT INTERESTED:**

```
You: "No worries at all. Out of curiosity, is that because you're happy
with your current agency, or is it just not the right time?"

[Listen to their answer]
```

**If happy with agency:**
```
You: "Fair enough. Most of our customers actually keep their agency too -
they just use us for overflow and save money where they can. If things
ever change, feel free to look us up. Have a good day!"
```

**If not the right time:**
```
You: "Understood. When would be a better time to revisit this - in a month
or two? ... Great, I'll make a note and reach out in [timeframe]. In the
meantime, I'll send you a quick email with our info in case you need
last-minute cover before then. What's the best email?"
```

---

**If they have questions/want more info:**

```
You: "Happy to explain. We're basically a platform that shows you local
care workers who are available for shifts. You post what you need - date,
time, rate - and workers apply. You see their full profile, experience,
everything, and you choose who you want.

We charge a 15% fee on top of the worker's rate, which ends up being about
20% less than most agencies. And the workers actually earn more, which
means better people want to join.

Does that make sense? Would it be worth a quick demo to see how it works?"
```

---

### Common Objections During Cold Calls

**"We're happy with our agency"**
```
"That's great - reliable agency relationships are valuable. Most of our
customers keep their agency too. They use us for the shifts their agency
can't fill, or when they want to save some money. Would it be worth having
us as a backup option?"
```

**"Send me an email"**
```
"Absolutely, I'll do that. What's the best email? ... Great. And just so
I send you the right info - what's your biggest frustration with staffing
right now? ... Got it. I'll include some specifics about that. Can we
book a quick call for [day] to walk through it together?"
```

**"We don't use agency staff"**
```
"That's impressive - your retention must be strong. What do you do when
someone calls in sick last minute? ... Interesting. Well, if you ever need
a backup option, we're here. Can I send you some info just in case?"
```

**"I'm too busy"**
```
"I totally get it - staffing headaches probably aren't helping. Quick
question: if I could save you 20% on agency costs and hours of phone calls
chasing cover, would 15 minutes be worth it? ... Great, let's find a time
that works. What does your week look like?"
```

**"How much does it cost?"**
```
"Good question. Workers set their own rates - usually around £14-17 per
hour for HCAs. We add a 15% platform fee on top, so your total cost is
typically around £17-20 per hour. Most homes save about 20% compared to
their agency. Does that sound in the right ballpark?"
```

### Voicemail Script (30 seconds max)

```
"Hi [First Name], this is [Your Name] from NewAgencyMarketplace. I work
with care homes in [Area] who are looking to fill shifts faster and spend
less on agencies. I'd love to see if we could help [Care Home Name].

You can reach me at [phone number] - that's [repeat phone number]. Or I'll
try you again tomorrow. Talk soon!"
```

**Tips:**
- Keep under 30 seconds
- Say phone number twice, slowly
- Sound upbeat, not scripted
- Follow up with an email same day

## Discovery Call Script

### Objective
Understand their situation, qualify them, and book a demo if they're a fit.

### Duration
15-20 minutes

### Structure
1. Rapport & Agenda (2 min)
2. Discovery Questions (8-10 min)
3. Brief Solution Overview (3-5 min)
4. Next Steps (2 min)

---

### Opening (2 minutes)

```
You: "Hi [First Name], thanks for making the time. How's your day going?"

[Brief small talk - 30 seconds max]

You: "Before we dive in, I want to make sure this is useful for you. I've
got some questions to understand your situation, then I can show you if
and how we might be able to help. If it's a fit, we can talk about next
steps. If not, no problem. Sound good?"

[Wait for agreement]

You: "Perfect. So tell me a bit about [Care Home Name]. How many beds,
what type of care, that kind of thing?"

[Let them talk - take notes]
```

---

### Discovery Questions (8-10 minutes)

**Ask these in a conversational flow, not like an interrogation:**

**Current Situation:**
```
"When you need temporary cover, what do you typically do?"

"Which agencies do you use?"

"How often do you need short-term or last-minute cover - we talking
weekly, a few times a month?"

"What's a typical shift look like - 8 hours, 12 hours?"
```

**Pain Points:**
```
"What's the most frustrating thing about using agencies?"

"Tell me about the last time you couldn't get a shift filled. What happened?"

"How much time do you spend each week chasing agencies or managing temp staff?"

"What do you pay per hour through your agency?" [Critical for ROI]
```

**Impact:**
```
"When you can't fill a shift, what happens? Do you go short-staffed or...?"

"How does that affect your team - the permanent staff who have to cover?"

"Have you had any CQC concerns about staffing consistency?"
```

**Decision Process:**
```
"If you found something that worked, who else would need to be involved
in the decision?"

"What would need to be true for you to try something new?"

"Is reducing agency spend something you're actively working on, or is it
more of a 'would be nice' thing?"
```

---

### Qualification Check

**Good fit signals:**
- Uses agency staff regularly (10+ shifts/month)
- Frustrated with cost, quality, or reliability
- Has authority to make decision or clear path to decision-maker
- Looking to solve the problem now (not "someday")

**Poor fit signals:**
- Rarely uses temporary staff (<5 shifts/month)
- Completely happy with current situation
- No authority and unclear on who does
- No budget or procurement locked in

---

### Solution Overview (3-5 minutes)

**Transition:**
```
You: "Thanks for sharing all that. Based on what you've told me, I think
we could definitely help. Let me quickly explain how it works, then we
can dig into specifics.

[Keep this brief - save details for the demo]

So NewAgencyMarketplace is pretty simple. You post a shift - date, time,
the rate you'll pay - and local care workers who are available apply to
work it.

You see their full profile before you accept anyone. Experience,
qualifications, availability, and ratings from other homes once we have
those.

We charge 15% on top of the worker's rate. So if they charge £15 an hour,
you pay £17.25. Based on what you said about paying £[their rate] through
agencies, you'd be saving about [X] per shift.

Over a month, with [Y] shifts, that's roughly £[Z] back in your pocket.

The real benefit though is the relationships. These are local people who
want to come back. After a few months, you've got a bank of regulars who
know your home."
```

---

### Handling Questions

**"What types of workers do you have?"**
```
"Right now, mostly HCAs and support workers with care home experience.
We're building out seniors and nurses too. In your area, we currently
have [X] workers available - I can show you profiles on the demo."
```

**"How do you verify them?"**
```
"Workers self-declare their DBS status and experience when they sign up.
You see all of that in their profile. Longer term, we're adding
verification badges, but honestly, you know within one shift if someone's
any good - and you don't have to use them again if they're not."
```

**"What if no one applies?"**
```
"Good question. We only launch in areas where we have enough workers to
be useful. In your postcode, we've got [X] available. For your first few
shifts, I'd recommend posting 24-48 hours ahead to get good coverage.
Emergency same-day shifts work too, but it depends who's available."
```

---

### Next Steps (2 minutes)

**If qualified and interested:**
```
You: "Sounds like this could be a good fit. The best next step would be
a quick demo where I can show you the platform and how many workers are
near you. Takes about 20 minutes.

Would [Day] or [Day] work? I can do mornings or afternoons.

[Book the demo]

Perfect. I'll send you a calendar invite with a video link. Before then,
I'll also send over a one-pager and the savings calculation we talked
about. Any questions before then?"
```

**If they need to involve others:**
```
You: "Makes sense. Would it be helpful to do the demo with [owner/director]
on the call too? That way you're both seeing it together and can discuss
right after.

If not, I can send you a summary and recording to share with them.

What works best?"
```

**If not a fit / not ready:**
```
You: "Sounds like the timing might not be right, and that's okay. What
I'll do is send you some info, and I'll check back in [timeframe]. If
anything changes before then - you're stuck without cover on a Friday
night - just give me a shout.

What's the best email to send that to?"
```

## Demo Call Script

### Objective
Show the platform, prove value, and close for sign-up or trial.

### Duration
20-30 minutes

### What You'll Need
- Screen share ready
- Platform open in browser (logged in)
- Their postcode ready to show local workers
- ROI calculator with their numbers
- Sign-up link ready to drop in chat

---

### Opening (3 minutes)

```
You: "Hi [First Name], great to see you again. Thanks for making time for
this. How's your week been?"

[Brief rapport - 1 min]

You: "So today I want to show you how the platform works and, more
importantly, how many workers are available near [Care Home Name]. Then
we can talk about getting you set up.

Before I share my screen - quick question: since we last spoke, has
anything changed? Any urgent shifts you're trying to fill?"

[Note: If they have an urgent need, address it immediately - this is a
live opportunity]

You: "Great. Let me share my screen and we'll dive in."
```

---

### Platform Walkthrough (10-15 minutes)

**Step 1: Show Worker Availability**
```
You: "First thing I want to show you - here's your postcode, [postcode].
These are the care workers available near you right now."

[Show the worker list/map]

You: "As you can see, we've got [X] workers within [Y] miles. Let me
click into one profile so you can see what you'd be looking at..."

[Show a profile]

You: "So here's [Worker Name]. You can see their experience - [X] years
in care - their qualifications, availability, and hourly rate. This is
all visible before you accept them.

Any questions about the worker profiles?"
```

**Step 2: Post a Shift**
```
You: "Now let me show you how quick it is to post a shift. I'll create a
test one.

[Navigate to 'Post Shift']

You: "So I put in the date - let's say next Tuesday. Start time... end
time... Role - let's say HCA. And the rate you'll pay the worker.

Now, what would you normally offer for an 8-hour shift?"

[Input their number]

You: "Okay, £[X] per hour. So our 15% fee makes your total cost £[Y] per
hour. For 8 hours, that's £[total].

What would that same shift cost through your agency?"

[Compare]

You: "So you're saving £[difference] per shift. Over a month with [Z]
shifts, that's £[monthly savings].

Let me add any requirements... dementia experience, moving and handling...
whatever you need. And... posted.

That took what, 45 seconds?"
```

**Step 3: Show the Worker View**
```
You: "Now let me show you what happens next. Workers in the area get a
notification. They see your shift, and if it works for them, they apply.

[Show worker view if possible]

You: "When applications come in, you get an email. You review, you choose,
done. No phone tag with agencies."
```

**Step 4: Confirmation Flow**
```
You: "Once you accept someone, both of you get a confirmation email with
all the details. Worker turns up, does the shift, everyone's happy.

Any questions about how it works?"
```

---

### Address Objections

**This is where concerns come up. Common ones:**

**"What if they don't show up?"**
```
"It's a valid concern. The difference is these workers have chosen your
shift specifically. They're not being sent by an agency because it's the
only work available. That said, if someone doesn't show, you don't pay,
and we help you find a replacement. Once you've got regulars, no-shows
become rare."
```

**"The savings aren't that big"**
```
"You're right that per-shift it's not huge. But two things: one, it adds
up over a month and year. Two, the bigger value is building relationships
with workers who come back. Regulars who know your home, your residents -
that's worth more than the financial savings."
```

**"I need to think about it / speak to my boss"**
```
"Totally understand. What specific questions do you think they'll have?
Let me help you answer those now so you go in prepared.

[Answer their questions]

And would it help if I put together a one-page summary with the ROI
calculation? You could forward that directly."
```

---

### Close (5 minutes)

**Trial Close:**
```
You: "So, [First Name], based on what you've seen - does this look like
something that could work for [Care Home Name]?"

[Wait for response]
```

**If YES:**
```
You: "Great! The best way to see if it works is to try it. Let me send
you the sign-up link right now - I'll drop it in the chat.

[Send link]

You: "If you create your profile today, you'll be all set to post your
next shift whenever you need one. And remember, your first 3 shifts are
with 0% fee - just pay the worker.

While we're on, do you want to set up your profile together? It takes
about 5 minutes."

[Walk them through sign-up if they agree]
```

**If MAYBE / NEED TO THINK:**
```
You: "No problem. What would help you make a decision?

[Listen and address]

How about this - I'll send you everything we discussed: the platform link,
ROI calculation, and a summary. You have a think, and I'll check in on
[Day]. Does that work?"
```

**If NO:**
```
You: "Appreciate you being direct. Can I ask what the main concern is?

[Listen]

[If it's something you can address, do so. If not:]

That's fair. Things change - if you ever need last-minute cover and your
usual options aren't working, keep us in mind. I'll send you the info
anyway, just in case."
```

---

### Wrap Up

```
You: "Thanks so much for your time today, [First Name]. To recap:

• You've got [X] workers available near you
• You'd save approximately £[Y] per shift / £[Z] per month
• Next step is [sign up / think about it / speak to boss]
• I'll follow up on [Day]

Any final questions? ... Great, talk to you soon!"
```

## Post-Call Checklist

After every call:

- [ ] Log call notes in CRM immediately
- [ ] Record outcome (meeting booked, callback scheduled, not interested)
- [ ] Send follow-up email within 2 hours
- [ ] Add to email sequence if no meeting booked
- [ ] Schedule next action (callback, send info, follow-up)
- [ ] Update contact information if needed
