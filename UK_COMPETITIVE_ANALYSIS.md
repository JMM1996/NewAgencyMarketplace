# UK Care Staffing Market - Competitive Analysis

> **Research Date**: January 2026
> **Purpose**: Identify competitors and strategies to win in the UK care staffing marketplace

---

## Executive Summary

The UK care staffing market is valued at **£12+ billion** with approximately **680,000 people employed by agencies** delivering direct care. There are **~110,000 vacancies** at any time, creating significant demand for efficient staffing solutions.

Your 15% fee model is **competitive** - traditional agencies charge 25-40% margins, while newer platforms like Florence and Staffscanner have disrupted this with lower-cost models.

---

## Direct Competitors

### 1. Florence (Market Leader)
**Website**: [florence.co.uk](https://www.florence.co.uk)

| Aspect | Details |
|--------|---------|
| **Model** | Pay-as-you-go marketplace connecting care staff with NHS & care homes |
| **Scale** | 100,000+ care professionals, used by NHS trusts and care homes |
| **Pricing** | No subscription; care homes set rates; platform takes margin |
| **Key Feature** | "Instant Pay" - workers paid same day after timesheet approval |
| **Claim** | Care homes save 30% on agency spend |

**Strengths**:
- Massive network of verified care professionals
- Florence Academy (free training courses, £2.99 for certificates)
- Bank Manager tool for internal staff banks
- Agency Manager for staffing agencies
- Strong NHS relationships

**Weaknesses (from Trustpilot reviews)**:
- Pay rates "hit rock bottom compared with other agencies"
- Strict penalties - workers blocked for single genuine mistakes
- IR35 changes forced move to PAYE only (no self-employed)
- Data privacy concerns raised by some users
- App controls which shifts workers can see based on tenure

**Opportunity for you**: Florence moved to **PAYE only** due to IR35, abandoning the self-employed carer market. Your introductory model (connecting parties who contract directly) could capture workers who want true self-employment.

---

### 2. Staffscanner
**Website**: [staffscanner.co.uk](https://staffscanner.co.uk)

| Aspect | Details |
|--------|---------|
| **Model** | Direct connection platform - "not a recruitment agency" |
| **Pricing** | Free for care workers; care homes pay per connection |
| **Pay Rates** | Min £10.50/hr (carer), £21/hr (nurse) |
| **Payment** | Within 48 hours of shift completion |
| **USP** | "Cutting out the middleman" |

**Strengths**:
- Lower fees than traditional agencies
- Immediate/last-minute shift alerts to nearby professionals
- Day, night, and live-in options
- Not VAT registered (cost advantage passed to clients)

**Weaknesses**:
- Smaller network than Florence
- Introduction fees if care home hires worker permanently (£2,000-£5,000)
- £50 cancellation fee within 24 hours

**Opportunity for you**: Your flat 15% is simpler and more transparent than their variable fees.

---

### 3. Curam Care (Closest Business Model)
**Website**: [curamcare.com](https://www.curamcare.com)

| Aspect | Details |
|--------|---------|
| **Model** | Marketplace for self-employed carers |
| **Fee** | **12.5% + VAT (~15% total)** - "lowest in the sector" |
| **Focus** | Live-in care, hourly care, private clients |
| **Geography** | All main UK postcodes |
| **Worker Control** | Carers set own rates, choose hours |
| **Example** | Carer sets £16/hr → takes home £13.60/hr |
| **Permanent Hire Fee** | £750 + VAT (after 90 days on platform) |

**Strengths**:
- True self-employed model (like yours)
- Transparent pricing (like yours)
- Clients pay 22% less than UK Home Care Association rate
- Nationwide coverage
- Focus on building carer-client relationships

**Weaknesses**:
- Primarily focused on **private/home care**, not care homes
- Less focus on short-term/emergency shift cover
- Focused on live-in care (£950-£1,400/week)

**Opportunity for you**: They've validated the 15% marketplace model works. You apply it to care homes (B2B) instead of private clients (B2C). Different market, same proven economics.

---

### 4. Hometouch
**Website**: [myhometouch.com](https://myhometouch.com)

| Aspect | Details |
|--------|---------|
| **Model** | Marketplace connecting self-employed carers with clients |
| **Quality Filter** | Only 5% of applicants accepted |
| **Minimum Rate** | £10/hr minimum, carers typically charge £15+ |
| **Worker Pay** | Carers take home over £12/hr on average |
| **Comparison** | Same carer via agency = £20+/hr to client |
| **Search** | Free for clients to search carer library |

**Strengths**:
- Rigorous vetting (5% acceptance rate)
- Accredited Living Wage employer
- Cost-effective due to no agency overheads
- Higher carer wages + lower client costs
- Offers both Introductory AND Regulated/Fully-managed care

**Weaknesses**:
- Focused on home/private care, not care homes
- Selective acceptance (5%) severely limits supply
- Carers cover own travel costs

**Opportunity for you**: Their strict vetting limits supply. A balance of quality checks without over-filtering could capture more of the market. Their ~25% margin is higher than your 15%.

### 5. Xpress Health
**Website**: [xpresshealth.co.uk](https://xpresshealth.co.uk)

| Aspect | Details |
|--------|---------|
| **Model** | Platform connecting care homes with vetted professionals |
| **Features** | Mobile app, instant shift posting, verification of credentials |

**Strengths**:

### 6. NHS-Focused Platforms (Adjacent Competitors)

#### Patchwork Health
- **Focus**: NHS hospitals, not care homes
- **Scale**: 100+ healthcare sites, 55,000+ workers
- **Funding**: £20M Series B
- **Model**: Collaborative staff banks across NHS trusts

#### Locum's Nest (Acquired by Aya Healthcare, June 2025)
- **Focus**: NHS trusts, GP practices
- **Scale**: 60,000+ healthcare professionals, 50 NHS trusts
- **Savings**: £750M saved for NHS since 2015
- **Model**: Digital platform for bank shifts

**Why these matter**: They've proven the tech-enabled staffing model works at scale. However, they focus on NHS clinical staff, not social care. The care home market is underserved by comparison.

|-------------------------------|---------------------|-------------|------------------|
| £25-35 (agency carer) | £9-12 | £13-23 | **52-65%** |

**Florence (Care Assistant)**


**Curam (Self-Employed Model)**
```
Client pays:        £16/hour
Carer receives:     £13.60/hour
Curam fee:          £2.40/hour (12.5% + VAT = ~15%)
```

**Your Model (15% Marketplace Fee)**
```
Care home pays:     £18/hour
Carer receives:     £15.66/hour (keeps 85%)
Platform fee:       £2.34/hour (15%)
```

### Why Your 15% Works

| Model | Care Home Pays | Worker Gets | Your Advantage |
|-------|---------------|-------------|----------------|
| Traditional agency | £28 | £11 | Care home saves £10/hr, worker earns £4.66 more |
| Florence (PAYE) | £18 | £12 (after tax) | Worker keeps more (self-employed tax efficiency) |
| Curam | £16 | £13.60 | Similar model - validates 15% is viable |

### Florence Pay Rates (for reference)
- Care assistant/support worker: **£11.50 – £28/hour**
- Social care nurses: **£22.50 – £50/hour**
- Rates set by care homes, not Florence

### Homecare Association Minimum Price (2025)
- Recommended minimum: **£32.14/hour** (England)
- Average local authority pays: **£24.10/hour** (27% below minimum)
- This gap creates pressure on margins industry-wide

## Market Gaps & Opportunities

### 1. Self-Employment Model Gap
**Florence abandoned self-employed workers** due to IR35 complexity, moving to PAYE only. This creates an opening:

- Many care workers **want** true self-employment (flexibility, tax efficiency)
- Your "introductory" model keeps workers self-employed
- Care homes contract directly with workers = clearer IR35 status

**Action**: Market heavily to workers frustrated by Florence's PAYE switch.

---

### 2. Care Home Focus Gap
Most well-funded platforms (Patchwork, Locum's Nest) focus on **NHS hospitals**, not social care homes.

- Care homes are underserved by tech solutions
- 680,000 agency workers in care = massive market
- Care homes face same staffing crisis but less tech adoption

**Action**: Position as "the Florence for care homes" but simpler and cheaper.

---

### 3. Simplicity Gap
Competitors have complex features:
- Florence: Training academy, bank manager, agency manager
- Patchwork: Collaborative banks, appraisal software
- CoolCare: Full care home management suite

Your MVP approach (just connect care home + worker) is **refreshingly simple**.

**Action**: Market the simplicity. "Post a shift. Get it filled. That's it."

---

### 4. Pricing Transparency Gap
Many platforms have opaque pricing:
- Variable rates based on location/demand
- Hidden fees (cancellation, introduction fees)
- Complex tiered structures

**Action**: Your flat 15% is a differentiator. Make it prominent.

---

### 5. Regional/Local Focus Gap
Most platforms try to be nationwide from day one.

**Action**: Consider hyper-local launch (e.g., one region) to build density and reputation before expanding.

## How to Beat the Competition

### Strategy 1: Own the Self-Employed Niche
| What | How |
|------|-----|
| **Target** | Care workers who left Florence after PAYE switch |
| **Message** | "Stay self-employed. Work directly with care homes." |
| **Legal clarity** | Clear introductory model = IR35 safe |
| **Marketing** | Facebook groups, care worker forums, word of mouth |

### Strategy 2: Undercut on Price
| Competitor | Their Take | Your Take | Your Savings |
|------------|-----------|-----------|--------------|
| Traditional agency | 25-40% | 15% | 40-60% cheaper |
| Florence | ~20-25% (estimated) | 15% | 25-40% cheaper |

**Message**: "Same quality staff. Lower fees. More money for care."

### Strategy 3: Speed to Fill
Care homes need **emergency cover** - often same-day or next-day.

| Feature | Benefit |
|---------|---------|
| Push notifications to nearby workers | Faster response |
| Simple one-tap apply | Reduce friction |
| SMS alerts (not just app) | Reach workers without smartphones |
| "Urgent shift" badge | Prioritize visibility |

### Strategy 4: Trust Through Simplicity
Don't try to out-feature Florence. Win on simplicity:

| Florence | You |
|----------|-----|
| Training academy | Not your problem (yet) |
| Bank manager | Not your problem (yet) |
| Agency manager | Not your problem |
| Complex onboarding | Simple sign-up |

**Message**: "We do one thing: connect care homes with care workers. Fast."

### Strategy 5: Worker-Centric Approach
Florence reviews show worker frustrations:
- Low pay rates
- Harsh penalties
- Account blocks

**Win workers by**:
- Fair treatment policies (no instant blocks)
- Transparent system (workers see all shifts, not algorithm-filtered)
- Faster payment (consider instant pay as Phase 4 feature)

### Strategy 6: Care Home Relationships
Build direct relationships with care home managers:
- Local sales outreach (phone, visit)
- Free trial periods
- Referral incentives
- Case studies from early adopters
